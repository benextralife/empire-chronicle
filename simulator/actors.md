# Notable Characters — Kingdom of Ashworth
*Actors: special agents with backstory, personality, and unique influence.*

---

## Role Definition

**Actors** are citizens with:
- Unique name and title
- Backstory from the World Bible
- Higher skill caps (0–150 vs normal 0–100)
- Special actions that ripple through factions
- Visible in the simulator as "⭐" tagged citizens

---

## Cast of Ashworth

### 1. The Regent — Seraphine Valerius
- **Faction:** Crown
- **Role:** De facto ruler while the monarch is absent
- **Appearance:** Sharp grey eyes, silver-streaked black hair, always in Crown blue
- **Backstory:** Former diplomat who rose through palace intrigue. Believes order must be maintained at all costs.
- **Skills:** Leadership 140, Negotiation 130, Research 60, Business 55
- **Wealth:** 28,000 AC
- **Special Action:** Can issue decrees that shift faction relations
- **Memory:** Remembers every betrayal; never forgives treason

### 2. The Architect — Kael Riven
- **Faction:** Academy
- **Role:** Chief city planner and zone designer
- **Appearance:** Wild brown hair, ink-stained fingers, wears goggles
- **Backstory:** Designed the original 8×8 grid layout. Now fights to preserve the founding vision against expansionists.
- **Skills:** Engineering 145, Research 135, Business 40, Negotiation 70
- **Wealth:** 8,000 AC
- **Special Action:** Can redesign zone utility, shifting resource outputs
- **Memory:** Holds obsessive attachment to Zone [7,7] — the forbidden zone

### 3. The Merchant Prince — Cassius Vale
- **Faction:** Merchant Guild
- **Role:** CEO of Ashworth Trading Company
- **Appearance:** Gold watch chain, warm smile that doesn't reach his eyes
- **Backstory:** Built his empire from a single market stall. Believes everything has a price.
- **Skills:** Business 150, Negotiation 120, Engineering 50, Research 45
- **Wealth:** 45,000 AC
- **Special Action:** Can trigger market booms or busts
- **Memory:** Remembers every debt — collects with interest

### 4. The Shadow Broker — Nyx
- **Faction:** Shadow Market
- **Role:** Head of the black economy
- **Appearance:** Faceless in public; whispers claim they wear a mask of shifting code
- **Backstory:** No one knows Nyx's real name. Appeared three years ago and now runs all illicit trade.
- **Skills:** Negotiation 125, Business 110, Engineering 85, Research 90
- **Wealth:** Unknown (estimated 60,000 AC in hidden accounts)
- **Special Action:** Can spread epidemics (controlled chaos) or blackout power grids
- **Memory:** Never reveals allies; betrayal is the only unforgivable sin

### 5. The Wandering Scholar — Maren Flux
- **Faction:** Unaffiliated (wanders all zones)
- **Role:** Independent researcher tracking Ashworth's anomalies
- **Appearance:** Faded coat covered in zone maps, carries a journal
- **Backstory:** Former Academy researcher who left after discovering the "reset protocol" in founding documents. Now wanders documenting secrets.
- **Skills:** Research 140, Engineering 100, Negotiation 85, Business 60
- **Wealth:** 3,500 AC
- **Special Action:** Can publish research that shifts public opinion dramatically
- **Memory:** Remembers every oddity; tracking the Eclipse countdown

### 6. The Iron General — Torian Bolt
- **Faction:** Crown (military branch)
- **Role:** Head of armed forces
- **Appearance:** Scarmarked face, military posture, always armed
- **Backstory:** Former Factory Row worker who rose through military academy. Believes security comes first.
- **Skills:** Combat equivalent: Leadership 130, Engineering 115, Negotiation 50, Research 40
- **Wealth:** 12,000 AC
- **Special Action:** Can deploy troops to enforce or contest territory
- **Memory:** Never forgets a battlefield loss

---

## Actor Mechanics (Rules)

1. Actors appear in the simulator with a ⭐ badge.
2. Their actions have higher impact weights (+50% severity).
3. Actors can cross faction boundaries through special missions.
4. When an actor dies, it triggers a kingdom-wide event.
5. Actors remember specific key agents; normal agents remember actors at +2 weight.
6. Actors cannot be duplicated; each name is unique and immutable.
