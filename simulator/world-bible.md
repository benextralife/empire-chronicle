# World Bible — Kingdom of Ashworth
*Version 1.0 — Lore & Culture Layer*

---

## Geography

### The Realm
Ashworth is an island nation, 64 zones arranged in an 8×8 grid.

- **Northern zones (rows 0–2):** Mountains and Academy research outposts. Cold, windy, thin air. The Academy faction controls most northern territory.
- **Central zones (rows 3–5):** Capital district, markets, government buildings. Crown territory. Highest population density.
- **Western zones (cols 0–2):** Merchant Guild trade routes and warehouses. Port access.
- **Eastern zones (cols 5–7):** Industrial factories and energy plants. Corp territory.
- **Southern zones (rows 6–7):** Farmland, reservoirs, and Shadow Market black markets. Lawless border.

### Key Locations
- **Ashworth Citadel** (center, zone [3,3]–[4,4]): Seat of Crown power
- **Academy Spire** (north, zone [1,2]): Research hub
- **Guild Docks** (west, zone [3,0]): Trade center
- **Factory Row** (east, zone [4,7]): Industrial zone
- **Bazaar of Whispers** (south, zone [6,4]): Shadow Market HQ

## History

### Era 1: Founding (Year 0–50)
The four founders established Ashworth as a neutral research and trade nation. The Crown provided governance, the Academy brought knowledge, the Merchant Guild built trade, and the Shadow Market emerged from necessity.

### Era 2: Expansion (Year 51–120)
Growth brought conflict. Territory disputes between Crown and Merchant Guild escalated into the Trade Wars. The Academy remained neutral, focusing on research breakthroughs.

### Era 3: Industrial Age (Year 121–200)
Factory Row transformed the eastern zones. Energy became a critical resource. The Shadow Market profited from resource scarcity.

### Era 4: Modern Ashworth (Year 201–Present)
The current era. All four factions coexist in an uneasy alliance. Public opinion shifts with each policy announcement. Birth rates rise in urban zones; disease outbreaks challenge healthcare.

## Culture

### Social Norms
- Cooperation is valued but competition is expected.
- Wealth brings respect; poverty brings suspicion.
- Academy members are seen as aloof but brilliant.
- Merchant Guild members are charming but ruthless.
- Crown officials are formal but distant.
- Shadow Market members are feared but necessary.

### Language
- Official: English
- Common slang: a mix of tech terminology and old-world courtesy titles
- "Ashworth" means "noble worth" in the founding tongue

### Festivals
- **Founding Day** (Day 1 of each year): All factions pause conflicts for celebration
- **Knowledge Gala** (mid-year): Academy showcases research
- **Trade Fair** (quarterly): Merchant Guild opens markets
- **Shadow Night** (annually): Black Market operates openly for one night

---

## Government

### System
- Constitutional monarchy with council of faction representatives
- Each faction appoints one council member
- Crown holds veto power
- Council votes on resource allocation and policy

### Laws (from Constitution)
- Agents have equal rights regardless of faction
- Territory disputes must be resolved through negotiation (preferred) or conflict
- Resource hoarding above 50,000 units is taxed
- Disease response is mandatory for all factions

---

## Economy

### Currency
- Ashworth Credits (AC)
- Backed by resource reserves
- Exchange rate floats with GDP

### Trade
- Merchant Guild controls official trade routes
- Shadow Market operates outside official channels
- Academy trades research for resources
- Crown taxes all trade

### Key Exports
- Research papers (Academy)
- Manufactured goods (Corp)
- Luxury goods (Merchant Guild)
- Information (Shadow Market)

## Technology

### Current Level: Industrial-Digital Transition
- Power grids: smart-grid capable
- Transportation: maglev trains in central zones
- Communications: encrypted mesh network
- Medicine: advanced diagnostics, limited epidemic response
- Research: quantum computing, fusion energy experiments

## Religion & Philosophy

- **The Ashworth Creed**: "Knowledge is the light, trade is the blood, power is the shield, shadow is the necessary dark."
- No central religion; philosophical factions exist.
- Academy followers worship knowledge itself.
- Crown adherents believe in order above all.
- Shadow Market believes in absolute freedom.
