# Global Literature Craft — World-Class Writer Master Reference

## What separates good writing from world-class writing
Nobel Committee: "a voice that I hear in the writing that I find within this particular writer's work and nowhere else."
Reddit concensus: "wisdom and emotional effectiveness is what elevates the great works above the merely technically proficient."

## Hemingway Iceberg Theory (Nobel 1954)
- **Understate, never overstate**: use a cold, subdued voice for highly charged situations. Flannery O'Connor: "if you want the reader to feel pity, be somewhat cold." The writer CANNOT bring emotion; the reader must bring it.
- **Show mental states through action only**: never say "she was sad." Show the action. Reader infers the emotion.
- **Omission**: know everything about the story, write very little. What you OMIT creates subtext and emotional weight.
- **Simplify**: "The higher processes of art are all processes of simplification." Hemingway/Cather/Maugham.
- **Mot juste**: the single, exact word. Every word must be necessary.
- **Objectivity**: state only facts. Facts create emotion; adjectives destroy it.
- **No moralizing**: do not preach. Let readers draw conclusions.

## Ishiguro / Murakami / Global Masters
- **Specific Materiality**: emotion grounded in physical detail — frayed cords, worn varnish, specific brands, textures.
- **Memory as Unreliable Cartographer**: memory is meaning-making, not accuracy.
- **Negative Space Pacing**: silences and corridors punch harder than monologues.
- **Micro-Attitude as Character**: people reveal themselves in emergencies through what they omit, not what they do.

## Harry Potter-Level Craft
- **Special Objects with Secret Burden**: hollow book, broken flute, worn notebook.
- **Threshold Rituals**: hidden shops, corner tables, dusk meetings.
- **Found Family through Moral Choice**: characters choose the hard right over easy wrong.
- **Humor Against Darkness**: dry, specific humor that makes darkness bearable.

## World-Class Mandatory Rules
1. No sentence longer than necessary.
2. No emotion state named directly.
3. Every scene must move plot OR reveal character.
4. Objects must carry emotional weight.
5. Voice must be unique but invisible.
6. Every word must be earned.
