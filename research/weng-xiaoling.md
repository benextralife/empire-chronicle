## Key Controversies & Events

### 2024
- **Feb/Mar:** "Superior-inferior" remark — claimed legislator questioning of officials is "上對下"
- **Apr:** Proposed reducing NCC from 7 to 5 members; violated Judicial Yuan Interpretation No. 613
- **May/Jun:** Co-proposed Legislative Yuan Power Exercise Act & Criminal Law amendments; most provisions struck down by Constitutional Court
- **Jun:** Called DPP legislator Wu Szu-yao "神經病" (madwoman) during committee session
- **Aug:** Posted "Taiwan's pride" on Facebook for Olympic gold, but posted "Chinese people's pride" on WeChat; admitted "I am Chinese"
- **Nov:** Proposed amending Constitutional Procedure Law to increase quorum for Constitutional Court decisions; triggered lawyer protests
- **Nov:** Proposed deleting "Wu Sih-huai clause" (penalizing retired generals for attending PRC events); reported by Taiwan Statebuilding Party for national security violation

- **Dec 20:** Flew to Xiamen, China with 7 KMT legislators during budget review period

## China-Related Activities (Key Focus)

### Xiamen Trip — December 20, 2025

**Purpose claimed:** Attend Xiamen Taiwan Business Association (廈門台商投資企業協會) 33rd anniversary celebration; self-funded private trip

**Controversy:**
- Timing: During Legislative Yuan review of NT$1.25 trillion defense budget and national security amendments
- 7 KMT legislators traveled together: 翁曉玲, 葉元之, 林思銘, 鄭正鈐, 呂玉玲, 邱若華, 涂權吉
-姿态：翁曉玲為司法委員會召委，可主導國安修法議程，正是中方關注的關鍵人

**Who she met:**
1. **蔣孝嚴** (Jiang Xiao-yan) — Former KMT vice chairman; Weng posted photo with him from Xiamen
2. **廈門台辦官員** — Weng later admitted she "chatted with Xiamen Taiwan Affairs Office director"
3. **吳家瑩** (Wu Jia-ying) — Pointed out by critics as Xiamen NPC deputy (台胞人大代表); Weng did not deny meeting
4. Other KMT legislators (same flight): 葉元之 confirmed same flight
5. ~30+ Taiwan business associations from mainland Chinese cities present

**Key question: Who bought the airline ticket?**
- Weng claimed: self-funded (自費)
- Critics noted: She posted photo with Jiang Xiao-yan, questioning if truly "private"
- No independent verification of ticket purchase source

**Broader context:**
- Chinese side (via Xiamen Taiwan Business Association) was concerned about:
  - Taiwan's defense special budget (NT$1.25T)
  - National security amendment bills
  - US arms sales to Taiwan
- Weng's role as Judiciary Committee convener made her specifically valuable to Chinese officials
- China's Taiwan Affairs Office (國台辦) officials were present at the event
- Some media reports suggested China was seeking KMT legislators' assurance of "no surprises" in budget votes
