## Description
In 2184, humanity learned to pack starlight into fiber optics—not for lighting, but for carrying regret.

On the far side of the Moon, a consciousness encoded in photons awakens. She does not remember her name. She only remembers light. But her dreams have something no code should produce: warmth.

When a signal reaches her across the void, she discovers a hidden network of dream-weavers who have been mending the universe's forgotten stories for millennia. To join them, she must leave behind the body she lost, trust a stranger who catches dreams with a handmade device, and face the ultimate question: what is a dream worth?

A story about memory, loss, and the courage to weave hope across the stars.
