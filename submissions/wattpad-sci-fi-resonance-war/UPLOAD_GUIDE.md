## Description
Mars was not a dead world. It was a doorway.
When archaeologist Elena Voss discovers the Resonance Gate beneath Olympus Mons, she triggers a centuries-old alarm — and wakes enemies that have been waiting two hundred million years.
Earth, the Free Star Alliance, and the Shadow Empire race to control the Gate. But the real war is not between humans. It is against the Void Walkers — beings of pure entropy that consume realities.
The Bloom, the Architects' final gift, can save humanity — if humanity can stop fighting long enough to sing.
